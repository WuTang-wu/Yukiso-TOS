# Privacy Policy

**Yukiso Applications** is operated by **Yukiso Studio** 

**Last updated:** November 3, 2026

This policy explains what information the Bot collects, why, and what you can do about it.

## 1. Information we collect

When you use the Bot to apply or communicate with us, we may collect:
- **Discord information:** your Discord user and ID, username.
- **Application content:** the answers, portfolio links, files, and other material you submit.
- **Messages:** messages exchanged between you and Studio staff through the Bot.

The Bot does not read or store messages unrelated to your application, and we do not collect payment information.

## 2. How we use it

We use this information only to:
- Receive, review, and respond to your application.
- Let you and Studio staff message each other about it.
- Keep basic records of hiring decisions and prevent abuse of the Bot.

We do not sell your information or use it for advertising.

## 3. Who can see it

Your information is visible to authorized Studio staff involved in reviewing applications. It is stored using the services that run the Bot (such as Discord and our hosting/database provider). We do not share it with anyone else unless required by law.

## 4. How long we keep it

We keep application data for **1 months** after your application is closed, then delete it, unless you ask us to remove it sooner.

## 5. Your choices

You can ask us at any time to:
- Tell you what information we hold about you.
- Correct or delete your information and application.

Contact us at **support@yukisostudio.com** and we will respond within a reasonable time.

## 6. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your information, but no online service is completely secure.

## 7. Children

The Bot is not intended for anyone under the minimum age required by Discord, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

## 8. Changes

We may update this policy. The "Last updated" date will change when we do.

## 9. Contact

Questions about this policy: **support@yukisostudio.com**
